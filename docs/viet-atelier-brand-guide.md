# VIET ATELIER — website brand reference

This is the working content and visual reference for the Viet Atelier website. It summarizes the supplied identity materials so the project can follow them without repeatedly relying on the Downloads folder.

## Source priority and audience

- The latest dated identity document, `20260719 NHẬN DIỆN THƯƠNG HIỆU.docx`, is the primary source for brand story, positioning, slogan, voice, and palette.
- `CHÂN DUNG KHÁCH HÀNG.docx` and `Những vấn đề cần tổ chức lại để định hướng đúng Viet Aterlier.docx` inform the consumer persona and editorial approach.
- The original website brief also asks for decorative lighting and wall art for hotels, resorts, restaurants, and interior professionals. Keep this hospitality and project inquiry path available alongside the newer home-living brand story.
- The other supplied identity document positions Viet Atelier as an art and decor partner for hospitality projects. Treat that as a complementary project-service context, not a reason to replace the newer consumer-facing slogan or story. If the company wants one audience to take precedence, confirm it before removing either path.
- Use only verified company, product, project, and service facts. Supplied stock/reference photos are not evidence of Viet Atelier products or delivered projects.

## Brand foundation

- **Brand name:** VIET ATELIER
- **Short name:** VA
- **Legal name in the latest identity document:** CÔNG TY TNHH VIET ATELIER STUDIO
- **Slogan:** “Cho những ngày muốn trở về.”
- **English adaptation:** “For the days you long to come home.”
- **Brand idea:** Decor objects inspired by Vietnamese nature, culture, and people, expressed in a contemporary design language.
- **Making approach:** Combine craft and technology, technique and aesthetics, with considered materials, colors, and proportions. Do not claim particular manufacturing methods or product materials unless the product data confirms them.
- **Vision:** Become a Vietnamese-identity decor brand chosen for its aesthetic, quality, and emotional value.
- **Mission:** Bring a Vietnamese spirit into modern living with objects made to be used and kept for a long time.
- **Difference:** A coherent, selective aesthetic; not chasing trends or trying to suit every taste.

## Customer insight

The supplied persona describes design-aware urban homeowners, typically age 30–45, who want a home with a personal point of view rather than a showroom-like copy. They find it difficult to choose among lookalike decor and value products that feel meaningful and work together over time.

> “Tôi không tìm một món đồ đẹp. Tôi tìm những món đồ giúp ngôi nhà kể đúng câu chuyện của mình.”

This is a persona insight, **not a verified customer testimonial**. Label it as “Customer insight” if shown on the site.

## Brand values

- **Gu thẩm mỹ:** Refined, harmonious, and practical to style.
- **Chế tác:** Care in the details.
- **Bản sắc Việt:** Inspiration from Vietnamese nature, culture, and people.
- **Luôn cải tiến:** Keep improving products and the customer experience.

## Visual identity

- Use the supplied `logo thương hiệu mới 1907.png` as the primary logo asset.
- The mark combines an Atelier-inspired “A” and a red brush stroke, expressing creativity and a handcrafted signature.
- **Red:** `#C62828` — creative energy and signature.
- **Black:** `#1F1F1F` — precise, confident, professional.
- **Warm white:** `#F8F6F2` — restrained, clear, contemporary.
- Keep the presentation minimal, with visual depth. Let the product, material, proportion, finish, and photography speak; avoid decoration that competes with the work.
- The earlier charcoal/ivory/champagne palette is superseded for this brand implementation by the supplied black/red/warm-white identity. Do not use champagne gold as the brand accent.
- Maintain readable contrast. Use red sparingly for emphasis and interaction states, not as long-form text.

## Voice and content

- Be concise, composed, and specific. Elegant appearance, restrained wording, careful detail.
- Put the object and its image first. Explain a story only when it helps people understand the object's character.
- Prefer the supplied vocabulary: *chế tác, bộ sưu tập, chất liệu, hoàn thiện, bản sắc, cảm hứng, tinh thần Việt, nghệ thuật ứng dụng,* and *không gian sống*.
- Avoid self-praise, generic marketing superlatives, trend-chasing language, and long process explanations.
- Use concise English adaptations rather than literal word-for-word translation.
- Product labels, descriptions, material, dimensions, availability, pricing, project names, and production claims must come from verified company content. Keep sample content visibly identified as a template.

## Website use

- Lead with the approved slogan and brand idea, not generic “luxury hospitality” claims.
- Keep decorative lighting and wall art as expandable product categories.
- Support both home-decor discovery and the previously requested hospitality/interior project quotation journey.
- Do not add checkout or payment. The inquiry form is a separate lead flow and must disclose whether delivery is configured.
- The supplied visual assets are references. Replace or approve them before implying they are Viet Atelier products or customer projects.
